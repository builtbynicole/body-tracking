Encrypted local storage

Your entries stay on this device. They are encrypted with your PIN before being stored.

About calorie estimates

Food estimates use an on-device reference set based on USDA FoodData Central. Exercise estimates use MET values from the 2024 Compendium of Physical Activities, time, intensity, and your latest weight. Both can differ from your actual energy intake or use.
