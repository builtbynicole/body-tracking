# Verdant V9 question inventory

This is the active Daily review sequence. A whole section can be skipped or opened directly from the section map. Blank optional fields mean “none.”

## Sleep — 5

1. About what time did you fall asleep?
2. What time did you wake for the day?
3. About how many minutes did it take to fall asleep?
4. About how many minutes were you awake during the night in total?
5. How would you rate the sleep quality? (1–10)

Removed after evidence/burden review: separate time into bed, separate time trying to sleep, number of awakenings, separate out-of-bed time, dream recall/details/tone, and a second wake-rested rating.

## Intake — 5

1. How well regulated was your appetite today? (1–10)
2. What did you eat today? (One card with Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks; include what and how much.)
3. How much water did you drink in total? (mL; Quick water is prefilled.)
4. Did you take Vitamin D? (Prefilled from Quick entry.)
5. Any other medication or supplements? (Optional, one per line: name — dose — time.)

## Activity — 5

Each is one optional card with activity name, minutes, and effort 1–10:

1. Strength training
2. Cardio
3. Flexibility or stretching
4. Dance
5. Chinese movement practice

## Care & upkeep — 5

1. Did you brush your teeth?
2. Did you floss?
3. Did you complete your face care? (Face washing and/or skincare.)
4. Did you shower?
5. Did you tidy your room?

## Body measurements — 2

1. Morning weight (optional weight in kg or 斤 plus time; prefilled from Log now.)
2. Evening weight (optional weight in kg or 斤 plus time; prefilled from Log now.)

Monthly body dimensions remain a separate periodic record because they do not belong in a daily sequence.

## Body events — 4 plus repeats

1. How many bowel movements did you have? (0–10.)
2. For each bowel movement: time, Bristol stool type 1–7, and difficulty/pain 0–10 on one card.
3. Did your period start today?
4. Did your period end today?
5. Any illness or sudden symptom? (Optional description and intensity 1–10.)

## Anything else — 1

1. Anything else you want in today’s body record? (Optional.)

## Separate periodic Physical function check

This is not part of the daily review:

1. Test date
2. 30-second chair-stand count
3. Timed Up and Go seconds
4. Highest CDC 4-stage balance position held
5. Hold time at that position
6. Chair sit-and-reach distance in cm
7. Resting breaths per minute
8. Whether a peak-flow reading is available; if yes, L/min
9. Optional context note
